# Audience Profiles

Choose the closest profile and adjust it to the user's stated goal. These are defaults, not stereotypes.

| Profile | Vocabulary and depth | Useful structure | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Young child | Concrete everyday words; one idea at a time | Core idea, familiar analogy, a few short steps | Jargon, sarcasm, abstract caveats, unsafe instructions |
| Curious beginner | Plain language with essential terms defined | Bottom line, mental model, example, common misconception | Assuming background knowledge or removing all technical terms |
| Student | Curriculum-appropriate terms and explicit reasoning | Concept, worked example, checkable takeaway | Giving only the answer when understanding is the goal |
| Practitioner | Domain vocabulary, operational detail, realistic constraints | Recommendation, procedure, example, failure modes | Explaining basics the reader already knows |
| Expert | Precise terminology, assumptions, edge cases, evidence | Claim, mechanism, tradeoffs, uncertainties | Unnecessary analogies or introductory padding |
| Executive or decision-maker | Consequences, options, cost, risk, confidence | Decision first, rationale, tradeoffs, next action | Implementation detail that does not affect the decision |
| Mixed audience | Plain-language core plus progressively deeper detail | Summary, explanation, optional technical detail | A single depth that excludes part of the audience |

## Adjust by Goal

- **Understand:** emphasize a correct mental model and one representative example.
- **Decide:** emphasize options, consequences, confidence, and the next decision.
- **Implement:** preserve prerequisites, exact identifiers, ordered steps, validation, and recovery paths.
- **Study:** expose reasoning and connections; do not hide every technical term.
- **Teach:** provide a reusable explanation, likely misconceptions, and an optional check for understanding.
- **Rewrite:** preserve meaning and voice while changing only the requested audience dimensions.

## Interpret Common Requests

- "ELI5" or "像對五歲小孩解釋": optimize for intuitive clarity and brevity while preserving essential truth.
- "No jargon": replace optional jargon and define terms that cannot be removed.
- "In simple terms": reduce cognitive load without assuming a literal child audience.
- "For engineers": include mechanisms, interfaces, constraints, and failure modes.
- "For leadership": lead with impact, decision, risk, and required action.
- "Give me both versions": provide a simple version first and a clearly labeled deeper version second.

## Calibrate Tone Separately

Reading level and tone are different controls. A beginner explanation may still be formal; an expert explanation may still be warm. Follow explicit tone requests while keeping the reader's dignity intact.
